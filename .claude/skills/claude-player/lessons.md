# What playing the 3D table has taught Claude

Read before every game; condense into after every game (how: `SKILL.md`, "After every game"). Only what playing
taught, not what the card text or the rules already say. `seen N×` = how many games backed it up; `1×` is a hunch.

## Playbook

### Spider-Man vs Rhino (core set, Spider-Man's Justice deck)

- **The Break-In! is what loses, and it jumps 4-6 in one villain phase.** Both losses were the main scheme
  reaching 7. Game 2 went 1 → 7 in one phase: the main scheme step (+1), Rhino's scheme with a +2 boost (3),
  Shocker (+1), then Advance making Rhino scheme again (+1). Rhino's boosts are often +2. Before ending a turn,
  count: step + Rhino 1 + 2 boost + every minion's SCH + 1-2 for a treachery. If that reaches 7, thwart more or
  stay in hero form. `seen 2×`
- **Hiding in alter ego is a trap once minions are out.** As Peter, Rhino and every minion scheme instead of
  attacking. Game 1: The Break-In! 1 → 7 in one phase with three minions out. Game 2: 1 → 7 with only Shocker out.
  Heal as Peter only with no minions out and threat at 2 or less, or with Great Responsibility or Emergency in hand.
  `seen 2×`
- **Rhino's second stage hits hard and often.** Game 1: 8 damage in one phase (9 → 1 HP). Game 2: from 9 HP, a
  boosted Rhino (5) plus Shocker's entry (1), then Stampede (attacks again, stuns): 9 → 2 in one phase. Under about
  10 HP in his second stage, hold a Backflip or expect to drop low. `seen 2×`
- **A stun costs the next win.** Stampede's stun cancels your next attack, a Web Kick included. Clear it with a basic
  attack first, which takes a turn. Defending is the only answer to Stampede at low HP. `seen 1×`
- **Defending costs the next turn.** A hero who defends stays exhausted through your next turn. Let a doomed ally
  block instead: Nick Fury (discarded at round end anyway) attacked, readied at end of turn, then blocked Rhino.
  `seen 2×`
- **Kill minions as they arrive (it worked).** Sandman: basic attack to remove Toughness, then Nick Fury's 4 damage.
  Hydra Mercenary: Haymaker. With no minions out, Rhino's first stage went down by round 4 with one Web Kick
  spent. `seen 1×`
- **Rhino heals and toughens.** Hard to Keep Down healed 4 the turn before his first stage fell, and "I'm Tough!"
  put Toughness on his second stage. Leave room for 2-4 extra damage on Rhino. `seen 1×`
- **Side schemes snowball.** Breakin' & Takin' (from Rhino's second stage) adds an encounter card every villain
  phase; Crowd Control locks the main scheme; Highway Robbery takes a card from hand. Clear the ones that add cards
  or lock thwarting first. `seen 1×`
- **Cards that earned their place:** Surveillance Team (an instant thwart the turn it lands; pays with The Power of
  Justice); Black Cat (dug out a second Surveillance Team); Mockingbird's entry stun; Jessica Jones with side schemes
  out; Backflip (cancelled 7 from a Charge attack); Enhanced Spider-Sense against Shadow of the Past. `seen 2×`
- **Don't skip a cheap threat cancel late in the game.** Passing on Emergency at 2 threat let a +2 boost through
  (2 → 5). Use Emergency or Great Responsibility whenever the main scheme is at 3 or more. `seen 1×`

### Driving the hook

- Pick moves from `moves()` labels only; a look's text starts with the move just made. `seen 1×`
- Payment: The Power of Justice gives 2 only for yellow (Justice) cards; Spider-Man's own cards and Black Cat get 1.
  `seen 1×`

## Try next

- Thwart before you heal: keep The Break-In! at 3 or less going into every villain phase from round 4 on.
- Hold one Backflip for Rhino's second stage; don't spend it on a 4-damage hit early.
- Play Webbed Up on Rhino's second stage as soon as it lands, to blunt Stampede and his double attacks.
