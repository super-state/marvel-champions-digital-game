# What playing the 3D table has taught Claude

## Playbook

### Spider-Man vs Rhino (core set, Spider-Man's Justice deck)

- **Rhino's second stage hits hard and often.** In one villain phase (round 2) Rhino, Shocker, Assault and Stampede
  dealt 8 damage between them (and Backflip stopped 4 more): from 9 HP to 1. Under about 10 HP in hero form with
  attack treacheries still in the deck, expect the worst. `seen 1×`
- **Defending costs the next turn.** A hero who defends in the villain phase stays exhausted through your next
  turn (cards ready only at the end of yours), so no basic attack or thwart. Defend when the damage saved beats a
  basic power; with an ally out, let the ally block the small hits. `seen 1×`
- **Hiding in alter ego is a trap once minions are out.** With Rhino plus two or three minions, each villain phase
  as Peter added 3 to 5 threat (main scheme step, every enemy's SCH, a Bomb Scare boost of +2), and The Break-In!
  went from 1 to 7 in one phase. Heal in alter ego only while the board is nearly empty. `seen 1×`
- **Minions piling up loses the game, not Rhino.** Both Swinging Web Kicks went into Rhino (his first stage fell in
  round 2) while Shocker, Hydra Mercenary (Guard) and Vulture stayed out and attacked or schemed every round. `seen 1×`
- **Side schemes snowball.** Breakin' & Takin' (from Rhino's second stage) adds an encounter card each villain
  phase; Crowd Control locks the main scheme; Highway Robbery took a card from hand (Backflip). Clear the ones that
  add cards or lock thwarting first. `seen 1×`
- **What worked:** Mockingbird's entry stun on Rhino cancelled his next attack; Jessica Jones thwarted for 3 with
  two side schemes out; Surveillance Teams cleared Crowd Control; Great Responsibility turned scheme threat into 1
  damage when threat was close to losing; Backflip saved 4 at 5 HP. `seen 1×`
