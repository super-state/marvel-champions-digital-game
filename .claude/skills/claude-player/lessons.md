# What playing the 3D table has taught Claude

Read before every game; condense into after every game (how: `SKILL.md`, "After every game"). Only what playing
taught, not what the card text or the rules already say. `seen N×` = how many games backed it up; `1×` is a hunch.
A rule misread in play is fixed in the hook's text (`play.ts`), not written down here: `look()` already says which
cards work in one form only, what each status does and the hand limit.

## Playbook

### Spider-Man vs Rhino (core set, Spider-Man's Justice deck) — 3 wins, 6 losses (plus one game lost to a reload)

- **The winning shape: allies on the table, Kicks saved for the second stage, then finish fast.** Game 8 won in
  round 7: Daredevil, Mockingbird and Jessica Jones out by Rhino's second stage, then Daredevil 2 + Kick 8 + Kick 8
  = 18 against his 15. Game 9 won in round 8: stage 1 was worn down by basics and allies (Black Cat, Jessica), the
  Kicks went only into stage 2 (one the turn it arrived, one the next). Game 4 won the same way with fewer cards.
  The allies do double work: damage, and blocking the attacks that would stun or kill Spider-Man. `seen 3×`
- **Let an ally take Stampede.** Its stun on Spider-Man would cancel the finishing Kick; Black Cat blocked it the
  phase before the win (game 9), as Jessica did in game 7. `seen 2×`
- **The last 7 HP of Rhino's second stage is where losses happen.** Games 3-6 all had Rhino's second stage on 7 by
  round 5-8 with no allies and one Kick, and stalled; in game 6 Hard to Keep Down healed him back to 11 twice. `seen 4×`
- **Two Kicks in one turn needs a double resource.** It came together in games 6 and 8: Genius (counts 2) plus
  Avengers Mansion and Spider-Sense draws. Hold Genius, Strength or Energy for that turn, and once two Kicks are in
  hand Spider-Man can defend freely (the Kicks don't need him ready). `seen 2×`
- **Count lethal before anything else.** Every turn, add basic + events + every ally's attack against Rhino's HP
  (and the Armored Rhino Suit's 5, Toughness, any Guard minion) before planning defence. `seen 2×`
- **The Break-In! is what loses, and it jumps 3-6 in one villain phase.** All four losses were the main scheme
  reaching 7. Rhino's boost is often +2; Bomb Scare adds +1 every phase; Hydra Bomber offers 2 damage or +1 threat.
  Before ending a turn, count: step (+1), Rhino 1 + 2 boost, every minion's SCH if you are Peter, 1-2 for a
  treachery. If that reaches 7, thwart more or stay in hero form. `seen 5×`
- **Crowd Control lands late, when it hurts most.** Game 5 was lost at 6/7 with it in play. Clear Crowd Control
  (and Breakin' & Takin') the turn they land whenever the main scheme is at 4 or more. `seen 2×`
- **Clear confusion early.** False Alarm came in four of seven games; its confusion sat on Spider-Man for most of
  game 5 and cost a For Justice! in game 4. A basic thwart while the scheme is low clears it cheaply (worked in game
  7). `seen 3×`
- **Threat discipline buys the time to win.** For Justice! paid with a mental or wild resource, Surveillance Team,
  Spider-Tracer on a minion you are about to kill (−3), Interrogation Room, allies thwarting. `seen 3×`
- **Alter ego is safe only with no minions out (a SCH-0 Mercenary is fine) and only in Rhino's first stage.** Then
  Peter dodges big attacks and heals (Recover, then flip back before the next big phase). With a scheming minion out,
  Peter loses to the scheme (games 1-3, 5). In the second stage Breakin' & Takin' deals an extra encounter card and
  every attack treachery (Stampede, Assault, Gang-Up) surges into another while you are Peter: game 7 went 1 → 7 in
  one phase as Peter with no minions out, five encounter cards in that phase. `seen 6×`
- **Rhino's second stage piles up fast.** One villain phase can bring Toughness, Hard to Keep Down (+4 HP; cancel it
  with Enhanced Spider-Sense), a Guard minion, Charge (7-10 ATK, overkill), Armored Rhino Suit, the nemesis (whose
  Sweeping Swoop stuns). `seen 5×`
- **Rhino hits hard.** Stage 2 or with Charge, expect 5-10 from one attack, plus Assault, Stampede or Gang-Up
  attacking again. Hold Backflip, a ready Spider-Man, a stun or an ally. Allies can't block Charge attacks
  (overkill). `seen 5×`
- **Stuns buy whole phases.** Webbed Up, and Mockingbird's entry stun (played as Peter it still waits for
  Rhino's next attack). `seen 4×`
- **Everyone readies at the end of your turn**, so attack with all, then block with all. But a Spider-Man who
  defends stays exhausted through his next turn: block with allies or Backflip when his action is needed. `seen 3×`
- **Kill minions as they arrive,** a Guard minion first. Order minion activations so Spider-Man defends the
  biggest. `seen 4×`
- **Cards that earned their place:** For Justice!, Surveillance Team, Daredevil, Mockingbird, Nick Fury (draw 3 in
  round 1), Black Cat, Jessica Jones, Avengers Mansion, Spider-Tracer, Backflip, Enhanced Spider-Sense. `seen 5×`

### Spider-Man vs Klaw (core set, same deck) — 0 wins, 2 losses (the win path checked from the `klawfinish` scene)

- **The threat clock is two stages: 6 then 8, with Klaw scheming 2 + a boost.** As Peter, one villain phase adds
  4-6, and the stage flip brings a minion. Both losses ended at the scheme or on a Peter who could not hide any more.
  Stay Spider-Man whenever Klaw is stunned (his attack is wasted) and thwart every turn. `seen 2×`
- **Defense Network is a crisis: no thwart reaches the main scheme until it is gone.** Clear it in the first two
  rounds (Daredevil 2 + a basic). `seen 1×`
- **Minions pile up: Klaw's deck is mostly minions.** Weapons Runner arrives from boosts, Masters of Mayhem makes
  every Master of Evil attack again, two Armored Guards (Guard + Toughness) can lock Klaw away. Kill minions as
  they land; Spider-Tracer + Interrogation Room on the kill is 4 threat. `seen 2×`
- **Pay Sonic Converter off the turn it lands** (energy + mental + physical, the move "Sonic Converter: Discard it").
  Its stun on every damaging Klaw attack cost Spider-Man three turns of attacks in game 2. `seen 1×`
- **Never let a script answer defend questions.** An auto-driver that declined every defend left Spider-Man on 1 HP
  in round 3 of game 1. Allies are blockers: they ready at the end of your turn, so attack with them, then block.
  `seen 1×`
- **Klaw (II) is 18 + 10 while The "Immortal" Klaw stands:** thwarting its 3 threat is cheaper than 10 damage.
  `seen 1×` (scene)

### Spider-Man vs Ultron (core set, same deck) — 0 wins, 1 loss

- **Drones are the whole fight, and they snowball.** Each is a card off your deck with 1 SCH, 1 ATK, 1 hit point.
  By round 3: four Drones, an Advanced Ultron Drone (Guard, 4 hit points, a new Drone when it dies) and two side
  schemes with hazard icons, so three encounter cards a phase. Kill every Drone the turn it lands. `seen 1×`
- **Interrogation Room is the card of the matchup:** every Drone broken is 1 threat off a scheme. Get it down in
  round 1 or 2, with allies to do the breaking. Spider-Tracer on a Drone is 3 threat for 1 damage. `seen 1×`
- **Never go Peter with more than two Drones out.** Every Drone schemes: six of them filled Countdown to Oblivion
  from 0 to 5 in one phase. That last stage's threat can't be removed, so Assault on NORAD (10) is the real clock;
  For Justice! belongs on NORAD. `seen 1×`
- **Upgraded Drones doubles the damage** (+1 ATK, +1 hit point each): pay it off the turn it lands (energy, mental,
  physical; a Web-Shooter's wild covers the missing one). A second copy came two rounds later. `seen 1×`
- **Ultron's and NORAD's "threat or a Drone":** a Drone costs a deck card and 1 health a round until it is broken;
  threat is cheaper while NORAD is under about 5 and Spider-Man is under 6. `seen 1×`
- **Invasive AI and Ultron's Imperative carry hazard icons** (one more encounter card each, every phase): clear
  the first the turn it lands. Invasive AI also threw Nick Fury, Surveillance Team and Strength off the deck. `seen 1×`
- **The deck runs dry by round 6** (Drones, Invasive AI, Avengers Mansion): the reshuffle is one more encounter card.
  `seen 1×`

### Captain Marvel (Leadership starter deck) — 0 wins, 3 losses (one each against Rhino, Klaw, Ultron)

- **She draws through her deck fast.** Commander every round as Carol, Rechannel, Alpha Flight Station (2 as Carol)
  and Maria Hill emptied the 40 cards by round 5 against Klaw; the reshuffle deals an encounter card facedown in
  front of her. Draw only what you will use. `seen 2×`
- **Recover as Carol, then flip to Captain Marvel the same turn** works (form change needs no ready hero), but she
  stays exhausted, so she can't defend that villain phase. `seen 2×`
- **Thwart before you build.** All three losses had the scheme or the minions run away while turns went on allies and
  upgrades: Rhino's Break-In! 4 → 8 in one phase, Ultron's NORAD 1 → 7 twice. Count the phase's threat first. `seen 3×`
- **Lead from the Front with four characters out is a big thwart turn** (+1 THW each: 10 threat off at once against
  Ultron). Keep it for that. `seen 1×`
- **Spider-Woman's confuse is worth most against a high-SCH villain** (Klaw SCH 2), played while you stay Carol. `seen 1×`
- **Cosmic Flight: spend it on the big hit.** Passing it on Rhino's 4 cost a game's worth of HP. `seen 1×`

### Free Play: Expert and the two other modules (Spider-Man, Justice) — 0 wins, 3 losses (one left unfinished)

- **Expert Rhino opens on stage II (15 HP, ATK 3) with Breakin' & Takin' already in, so its hazard deals two encounter
  cards from round 1.** Clearing it in round 1 (Nick Fury 2 + Mockingbird 1) paid off. Stage III is 16 HP, ATK 4,
  Toughness, and stuns on arrival: don't finish stage II with Spider-Man exhausted and Charge on Rhino. `seen 1×`
- **Legions of Hydra snowballs threat on two fronts.** Madame Hydra can't be damaged (or targeted by a damage-only
  card) while Legions of Hydra stands, and every time she schemes or attacks it gains 2; its hazard icon is a second
  encounter card each phase. Thwart Legions down first; Jessica Jones (+1 THW per side scheme) is the card for it.
  `seen 1×`
- **Hydra Soldier is a Guard whose death deals you an encounter card** in your own turn: kill it when you can afford
  the extra card. `seen 1×`
- **Biomechanical Upgrades with no minion out just surges** (into Crowd Control, here). `seen 1×`
- **Sonic Boom and other "spend X or …" cards:** the hook's moves don't show a card's resource icons; pick the
  resource cards (Energy, Genius, Strength) you can name. The table now refuses picks that don't pay. `seen 1×`

### She-Hulk (Aggression starter deck) — 0 wins, 4 losses (Rhino twice, Klaw, Ultron)

- **Her first turn hits hard:** Superhuman Strength as Jennifer, flip ("Do You Even Lift?" 2), attack 5 (stuns), Uppercut
  5 took Rhino from 14 to 2 in round 1. Open like that whenever the cards allow. `seen 2×`
- **Gamma Slam scales with her damage:** at 11 damage it hits for 11. Keep it for when she is low, and count it into lethal. `seen 1×`
- **Confusion stays on her across the flip.** A thwart event as Jennifer (Legal Practice) only cleared it and cost three
  cards. Read her statuses before a thwart in either form. `seen 1×`
- **Every loss was the scheme while she hit the villain.** Rhino's Bomb Scare + Breakin' & Takin' and Klaw's Defense
  Network piled threat faster than "I Object!" (1) and Superhuman Law Division (2) take off. Thwart before stage 2. `seen 3×`
- **An auto-player loses these games:** it passed Emergency and "I Object!" at 6/7 and lost. Play the villain phase's
  offers by hand. `seen 2×`

### Built decks from the bench (Spider-Man with Leadership, Captain Marvel with Justice) — 0 wins, 2 losses (Rhino)

- **Both took Rhino's first stage fast, then lost in stage two to two attacks plus a Stampede in one phase.** Spider-Man
  (Leadership) fell in round 8, Captain Marvel (Justice) in round 6; Breakin' & Takin' was never cleared in either.
  Clear it the turn stage two arrives (Jessica Jones, For Justice!, Nick Fury's 2 threat). `seen 2×`
- **Allies win stage one.** Vision with an energy card (+2 ATK), Mockingbird's stun, Nick Fury's 4 damage and both
  their attacks dropped Rhino's 14 by round 3 (Spider-Man) and round 5 (Captain Marvel). `seen 2×`
- **A stun stops the attack only: a stunned Rhino still schemes** while you are in alter ego (Captain Marvel game:
  Break-In 3 → 6 in a phase spent as Carol while Rhino was stunned). Confusion is what stops a scheme. `seen 1×`
- **For Justice! paid with a mental resource is 4 threat** and saved Captain Marvel at 6/7. `seen 1×`
- **Spider-Man with Leadership has no Web Kick turn to spare:** Make the Call needs an ally in the discard, so both
  copies are dead in the opening hand; mulligan them. `seen 1×`

### Driving the hook

- Pick moves from `moves()` labels only; a look's text starts with the move just made. `seen 1×`
- Several cards can wait to be read at once: loop on "Continue (after …" before a planned sequence. `seen 3×`
- When a scripted sequence prints only part of a reply, it hides what you need: print the whole look after every
  villain phase (game 6's winning double Kick fizzled on a stun the look showed), and keep each reply's "What
  happened" lines (game 7's losing phase can't be told card by card because a script kept only first lines). `seen 2×`
- Remote play (curl): a move's words with an em dash or other non-ASCII letters get mangled by the Windows shell;
  match on plain ASCII words ("Pay with Web-Shooter"). `seen 1×`
- An action ability that costs resources (Tenacity, paying Upgraded Drones off) ends with a "Use" move after
  paying, not "Play". `seen 2×`
- A hidden Browser pane stops drawing frames, so the table hangs on "playing something out". Hold the `?remote` page
  in a headless Playwright chromium (`--use-angle=d3d11 --enable-gpu`) on your own port instead; it plays at full
  speed. `seen 1×`
- A driver loop that answers defend questions must defend: one that declined left Spider-Man on 2 HP (Doomsday
  Chair game, as in the Klaw game before). `seen 2×`
- A deck built at the bench in a page of your own: hold the `?remote` page in headless Playwright with the saved decks
  preloaded into its storage (`mc-table-decks-v1`, and `battle-deck:<hero>` set to the deck's id), and the deal
  picks it up; the matchup line names the deck. `seen 2×`
- Read the whole hand before a villain phase: two Backflips sat unread while Drones hit for 1 each (Ultron game).
  `seen 1×`
