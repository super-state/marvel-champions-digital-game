# What playing the 3D table has taught Claude

Read before every game; condense into after every game (how: `SKILL.md`, "After every game"). Only what playing
taught, not what the card text or the rules already say. `seen N×` = how many games backed it up; `1×` is a hunch.

## Playbook

### Spider-Man vs Rhino (core set, Spider-Man's Justice deck)

- **The Break-In! is what loses, and it jumps 3-6 in one villain phase.** All three losses were the main scheme
  reaching 7. Rhino's boost is often +2. Before ending a turn, count: step (+1), Rhino 1 + 2 boost, every
  minion's SCH if you are Peter, 1-2 for a treachery. If that reaches 7, thwart more or stay in hero form.
  `seen 3×`
- **Threat discipline works.** Game 3 kept The Break-In! at 0-2 into nearly every villain phase (For Justice! paid
  with a wild or mental resource removes 4; Surveillance Team; allies thwarting) and lasted to round 10, against
  rounds 5 and 7 before. `seen 1×`
- **Hiding in alter ego is a trap once minions are out.** As Peter, Rhino and every minion scheme instead of
  attacking. Game 1: 1 → 7 with three minions. Game 2: 1 → 7 with Shocker. Game 3: 2 → 7 with Sandman and a Hydra
  Mercenary. Peter is safe only with no minions out and threat at 2 or less. `seen 3×`
- **Great Responsibility is a Hero interrupt: it does nothing while you are Peter.** Game 3 was lost counting on it
  as Peter. Emergency works in either form. `seen 1×`
- **Rhino's second stage piles up fast; finish him in one turn.** In game 3 Rhino was on 7 with a Kick in hand. One
  villain phase then brought "I'm Tough!", a Hydra Mercenary (Guard), Vulture and Highway Robbery, and the kill
  never came. Breakin' & Takin' deals two encounter cards a phase. Save resources to hit his second stage for 15 in
  one turn (two Kicks = 6 resources) instead of Kick, wait, Kick. `seen 2×`
- **Rhino heals and toughens.** Hard to Keep Down heals 4; "I'm Tough!" puts Toughness on either stage; Armored
  Rhino Suit takes all the damage (a whole Web Kick) until it holds 5. Pop the suit or the Toughness with a basic
  attack before a Kick. `seen 3×`
- **Rhino hits hard.** Stage 2 or with Charge, expect 5-7 from one attack, plus Assault or Stampede attacking again.
  Under about 10 HP, hold Backflip, a ready Spider-Man to defend, or an ally to block. `seen 3×`
- **Defending costs the next turn, except when the finisher is an event.** A Web Kick doesn't need Spider-Man
  ready, so defend freely the turn before a Kick finish. Doomed allies (Nick Fury, 1-HP allies) make good blockers.
  `seen 3×`
- **Kill minions as they arrive.** Sandman: basic attack for the Toughness, then 3+ damage. Bomber, Shocker,
  Mercenary: a basic or ally attack. Spend a Kick on a minion only to survive (it killed Vulture in game 3).
  `seen 2×`
- **Stuns buy whole phases.** Webbed Up on Rhino cancelled one attack and its stun cancelled the next (a
  Stampede). Mockingbird's entry stun did the same. `seen 2×`
- **Cards that earned their place:** For Justice!, Surveillance Team, Avengers Mansion (found both Kicks in game
  3), Web-Shooter (a wild resource each turn, Spider-Man only), Black Cat, Jessica Jones with side schemes,
  Backflip, Enhanced Spider-Sense against Shadow of the Past. `seen 3×`
- **Use Emergency when the main scheme is at 2 or more.** Passing at 2 let a +2 boost through twice (2 → 5).
  `seen 2×`

### Driving the hook

- Pick moves from `moves()` labels only; a look's text starts with the move just made. `seen 1×`
- Payment: The Power of Justice gives 2 only for yellow (Justice) cards. Web-Shooter's wild resource works only as
  Spider-Man. `seen 2×`
