# What playing the 3D table has taught Claude

Read before every game; condense into after every game (how: `SKILL.md`, "After every game"). Only what playing
taught, not what the card text or the rules already say. `seen N×` = how many games backed it up; `1×` is a hunch.

## Playbook

### Spider-Man vs Rhino (core set, Spider-Man's Justice deck) — 1 win, 3 losses

- **Count lethal before anything else.** Game 4 was won from a hopeless board (threat 6/7, Rhino on 10 ATK with
  overkill) because Rhino had exactly 11 HP + Toughness and no Guard minion out: basic attack (Toughness off), Web
  Kick 8, Daredevil 2, Mockingbird 1. Every turn, add up basic + events + every ally's attack against Rhino's HP
  before planning defence. `seen 1×`
- **The Break-In! is what loses, and it jumps 3-6 in one villain phase.** All three losses were the main scheme
  reaching 7. Rhino's boost is often +2; Bomb Scare adds +1 every phase. Before ending a turn, count: step (+1),
  Bomb Scare, Rhino 1 + 2 boost, every minion's SCH if you are Peter, 1-2 for a treachery (Advance). If that reaches
  7, thwart more or stay in hero form. `seen 4×`
- **Threat discipline buys the time to win.** Keeping The Break-In! at 0-2 (For Justice! with a mental or wild
  resource removes 4; Surveillance Team; allies and Daredevil thwarting) took games 3 and 4 to round 10. `seen 2×`
- **Alter ego is safe only with no minions out.** As Peter, Rhino and every minion scheme: 1 → 7 in games 1-3.
  Game 4 used Peter well while only a SCH-0 Mercenary was out, to dodge 9-10 damage Charge attacks. `seen 4×`
- **Hero interrupts do nothing as Peter:** Great Responsibility, Enhanced Spider-Sense, Spider-Sense's draw. They
  are only payment there. `seen 2×`
- **Confused cancels your next thwart, events included.** For Justice! fizzled in game 4. Clear confusion with a
  basic thwart first. `seen 1×`
- **Rhino's second stage piles up fast.** One villain phase can bring "I'm Tough!", Hard to Keep Down (+4 HP), a
  Hydra Mercenary (Guard), Charge (twice in game 4: 10 ATK, overkill) and the nemesis. Two Kicks in one turn needs 6
  resources, more than a 5-card hand gives (hand limit 5), so plan Kick plus allies plus basic. `seen 3×`
- **Rhino heals and toughens.** Hard to Keep Down heals 4; "I'm Tough!" on either stage; Armored Rhino Suit takes
  all damage until it holds 5. Pop the suit or the Toughness with a basic attack before a Kick. `seen 4×`
- **Rhino hits hard.** Stage 2 or with Charge, expect 5-10 from one attack, plus Assault or Stampede attacking
  again. Hold Backflip, a ready Spider-Man, or a stun. Allies can't block Charge attacks (overkill comes through).
  `seen 4×`
- **Stuns buy whole phases.** Webbed Up cancels one attack and its stun the next; Mockingbird's entry stun cancelled
  a 10-damage Charge attack in game 4. Charges stay on Rhino through a stun. `seen 3×`
- **Defending costs the next turn, except when the finisher is an event.** Doomed allies (Nick Fury, 1-HP allies)
  make good blockers. `seen 4×`
- **Kill minions as they arrive,** and kill a Guard minion with whatever it takes; Spider-Tracer on it turns the
  kill into −3 threat. `seen 3×`
- **Cards that earned their place:** For Justice!, Surveillance Team, Daredevil, Mockingbird, Nick Fury (draw 3 in
  round 1), Avengers Mansion, Web-Shooter (Spider-Man only), Black Cat, Jessica Jones, Backflip. `seen 4×`

### Driving the hook

- Pick moves from `moves()` labels only; a look's text starts with the move just made. `seen 1×`
- Several cards can wait to be read at once: loop on "Continue (after …" before a planned sequence, or every move in
  it fails. `seen 1×`
- Payment: The Power of Justice gives 2 only for yellow (Justice) cards. `seen 2×`
